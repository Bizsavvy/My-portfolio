## 3. What works

1. **Economics as the spine**: few designers can show a break-even model turning into interface rules; fintech hiring managers will notice.
2. **The number I got wrong**: owning a mistake, with the fix and its cost, is the most trustworthy section in the case study.
3. **Honest about what is unknown**: Impact says nothing is measured and names the number that would prove it, which reads as maturity, not weakness.

## 4. What isn't working

### Severity: Medium: No evidence from people
- **Evidence:** "nothing has been measured with real shoppers"
- **Why it matters:** At Mid this is acceptable when honest, which it is, but it now holds Research, Outcomes and the Strong rating.
- **Fix:** A light test of the cart nudges with a handful of people, reported plainly.

### Severity: Low: The model is not linked
- **Evidence:** "With roughly ₦5.4M in monthly fixed costs per dark store" (detailed page)
- **Why it matters:** The whole case rests on the model; an interviewer will ask where the inputs come from.
- **Fix:** Link or excerpt the model.
