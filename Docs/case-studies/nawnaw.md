# NawNaw

A quick-commerce app named after its own promise, with every screen traced back to a person who was simply *out of time* .

The problem

Sometimes the shops aren't open yet. Sometimes they are and you still can't get there, because what you're short of is time, not access. Next-day delivery is no use to someone who needs it in twenty minutes.

The outcome

15 mindelivery promise, made accountable on screen

₦10,000target basket the whole store is designed around

90/dayorders to break even per dark store
